# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Pokemon players and completionists who use a phone, tablet, or desktop alongside their games to track story progress, Pokedex completion, events, collectibles, and post-game goals across multiple titles.

## Product Purpose

PokemonPGC is a practical companion for recording detailed completion progress and carrying it across sessions and devices. Success means users can quickly understand what remains, mark progress without friction, and trust that their carefully recorded state is preserved.

## Brand Personality

Focused, playful, and dependable. The interface should feel recognizably inspired by Pokemon collecting and trainer tools without becoming a novelty interface or competing with the user's checklist work.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS surfaces that remove the product's game-specific character.
- Overdecorated gaming interfaces with neon effects, excessive motion, or unfamiliar controls.
- Dense interfaces that hide essential actions or make completion state difficult to scan.
- Visual treatments that imitate official Pokemon branding or imply commercial affiliation.

## Design Principles

- Keep progress legible: completion state and the next useful action should be easy to find.
- Protect collected work: syncing, importing, and account actions must communicate their state and failure behavior clearly.
- Use familiar controls: playful character belongs in details, not in reinvented form and navigation patterns.
- Work beside the game: mobile and desktop layouts must remain usable at a glance while the user is actively playing.
- Add personality with restraint: Pokemon-inspired cues should support hierarchy and recognition rather than decorate every surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, browser zoom, reduced-motion preferences, and screen-reader status announcements. Interactive targets should be approximately 44 to 48 CSS pixels where practical, and core tasks must work without hover or color alone.
